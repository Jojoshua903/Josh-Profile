How to play

1 · Place heroesPick a hero from the shop (keys 1–9, 0, - and = for the first twelve). Heroes are grouped into 6 categories; click the icons at the top of the shop to switch and click the ground next to the road. Shift+click places several. Otis, Guiseppe, Josh and Deniz can float on water.
2 · Start wavesPress Space or ▶. Enemies follow the road to the WIDR gate. Every one that gets through costs lives. The game saves after every wave, so you can continue later.
3 · UpgradeClick a hero, then use the panel or , . / to upgrade. Every path has 5 tiers. Crosspathing is 5-2-0: one path up to tier 5, a second up to tier 2, in any mix. Tiers 4 and 5 are Paradise upgrades. Press T for the full skill tree.
Main heroChoose one before each game. The first one you place earns XP from pops and waves, levels up to 10 and gets stronger, with ghost sight at level 5 and an aura at level 10.
AbilitiesTier 3 of every Path 3 unlocks an ability. Trigger it from the bar on the map or with Q W E R Y U I O P. Tiers 4 and 5 of Path 3 make the ability stronger.
4 · Know your enemiesArmor ignores weak hits. Ghosts need ghost sight. Frozen needs blasts, lasers, lightning, drones or abilities. Flyers need heroes that hit air. Shields block hits from the front. Medics heal and Sprouts regrow.
Map hazardsLava vents knock nearby heroes out, hotel doors hold enemies back, and the stoep tram flattens anything on the tracks.
Levels & unlocksEvery wave gives XP. Every level on the Season Pass (Season 1: Humble Beginnings) unlocks something new: skins, effects, music, titles and maps. Wins, events and achievements give Biscotti 🍪 for the Trophy Store.
