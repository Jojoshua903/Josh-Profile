🎭 Characters

Your cast of Ultimates. Give each a name and talent, then build them a body — pick an outfit, hair, skin tone and accessory — or use an emoji face or your own uploaded art. Facial expressions are drawn for each emotion.

🔸 Truth Bullets

Evidence the player fires at contradictions. Write the name, a short description, and mark which debate weak points each bullet correctly refutes.

💬 Trial Script

Build the flow of the trial. Add dialogue beats and Nonstop Debates in any order. Drag using the arrows to reorder segments.

🎵 Music Editor

Every song is generated live in the browser (no files needed). Preview them here, then assign a track to any script segment in the Trial Script tab. The music changes automatically as the trial reaches each segment.
🎧 Assign songs per segment in the Trial Script tab — each dialogue, debate, and showdown has a "Music" dropdown. Whatever is playing carries forward until the next segment sets a new track.

🪑 Courtroom Seating

Arrange your cast around the trial circle. During play, when someone speaks you'll see the students seated on either side of them, and the camera pans across the ring during Nonstop Debates.
Order = clockwise around the circle. The first and last seats are neighbors (it's a ring). Reorder with the arrows.

🔎 Investigation Maker

Build explorable crime scenes. Place objects to examine, cast members to question, and doors between rooms. In play, the student walks around, gathers clues, and picks up Truth Bullets before the trial. Leave this off (or empty) to skip straight to the trial.
Pick a room to edit its layout below. Doors you add can lead to any other room.
