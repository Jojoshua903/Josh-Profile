Your climber

Pick Your climber on the main menu, or open the gear locker at Base Camp. Change your body colour and build, eyes, mouth, brows and cheeks, outfit and colours, hat, scarf, backpack and goggles. Drag the preview to spin it, or hit Randomise for a surprise. Your climber blinks, grins when waving, pants when tired and goes cross-eyed when the Shadow takes them.

Base camp

Every session starts on the deck at Base Camp. Walk up to something and press E: the gear locker changes your jacket, hat, scarf, backpack and goggles; the trail board sets how fast the Shadow moves and whether you get the daily maze; the radio shows your room and copies an invite link; the tripod takes a group photo that gets pinned to the memories board; the benches let you rest by the fire.

Slot 2 is a snowball at camp. Hit the three bells past the railing, or boost a friend up the practice boulder to ring the one on top. When the whole team is standing inside the gondola, it counts down from 5 and carries you to the maze. After every round you ride back down.

Training trail

Base Camp has six practice stations: the sprint lane, the chalk slate, the dark shed (throw a glowstick in), the practice gate (plates or crank), the shadow puddle and the boulder summit. Each one stamps your Climber's Passport, shown top right at camp. Your very first climb in solo or local co-op is a gentle one: easy pace, a 60 second head start and a single stone gate.

Climbing

Rock steps block some corridors: boulders in the frost ring, ice ledges in the ice ring and toppled blocks in the temple. Look for the hand-holds. Jump at one and keep holding jump to climb up; reach the lip and you haul yourself over. Climbing drains stamina, and if it runs out you lose your grip and drop. A friend can also boost you straight onto the top. Rock steps show up on your trail map as little brown triangles.

Finding the way

Stacks of stones with a gold cap are trail cairns. They sit just past a junction on the true route to the sanctuary, so if you see one, that branch is right. Your trail map (slot 4) fills in the maze around you as you walk, draws a dotted line of where you have been, shows cairns you passed and lets you drop up to nine numbered pins. On Gentle you get plenty of cairns and the map reveals two tiles around you; on Standard fewer cairns and one tile; on Brutal only a few cairns near the start and the map just shows your own footsteps.

Survival tips

The Shadow Fog wakes after a head start (45, 30 or 20 seconds depending on pace) and closes on the centre over several minutes. Inside it you can't catch your breath, and if your stamina hits zero there you collapse after a few heartbeats. Get out fast.

Stone doors block the way in. Two climbers must stand on both pressure plates until the door is fully up; step off early and it slams. Alone? Hold E at the crank lever instead: slower, and it eats your stamina.

Online, voices carry about 15 m and get muffled by walls. Anyone in the fog sounds warped and echoey. M mutes your mic.

Chalk drops arrows on floors and crosses on walls. Glowsticks bounce and light dark corners. A boost launches a friend onto a rock step without climbing.
