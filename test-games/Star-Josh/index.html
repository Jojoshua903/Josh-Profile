Silver rings repair 25 shield. Gold rings repair 45 and give 500 points.

Blue crystals upgrade your shot: single, double, then triple.

Orange crystals fill your special meter at once. The meter also fills over time and with every kill.

Every fighter has a passive too. Check the character select screen before you launch.

You start with 4 fighters. Everyone else is unlocked through challenges. Check a locked fighter on the select screen to see what it takes.

Meta AI will interrupt you. Constantly. You can make it shut up in Settings.
