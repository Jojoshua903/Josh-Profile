Tips

  • Drift: keep drifting until the sparks turn blue, then yellow, then orange. Let go for a speed boost.
  • Rocket start: press and hold accelerate when the countdown shows 2 for a big boost at GO. Press on 3 and your engine stalls. Press on 1 for a small boost.
  • Jumps: yellow-and-black ramps launch you into the air. Press Shift while flying to do a trick and get a boost when you land.
  • Gliders: blue-and-white ramps on hilltops launch you over a gap in the road and your glider opens. Steer while you fly, hold ↓ to dive. Fall in and you get fished back out.
  • Walls: blue anti-gravity sections tilt the road onto the walls. Bump other racers there for a speed boost.
  • Loops: blue loop-de-loops take you upside down. You keep your speed all the way round, so just steer and enjoy the ride.
  • Boost pads: drive over the orange arrows.
  • Ice: your kart keeps sliding after you turn, so start turning early.
  • Items: grab a ? box and the roulette spins. Racers further back get stronger items. Press Space to use (or to stop the roulette early).
  • 🪙 small boost · 🍌 / 🍌×3 drop behind · ❓ fake box trap · 🐢 bounces straight ahead · 🐚 / 🐚×3 home in on the racer ahead · 📯 blasts everything around you, even Spiny Shells
  • 🍄 / 🍄×3 boost · golden 🍄 unlimited boosts for 7s · 💣 bomb · 🦑 inks the screens of everyone ahead · 👻 turn invisible and steal an item · ⭐ invincible · ⚡ shrinks everyone else · 🚀 Bullet Bill drives for you · 🔵 Spiny Shell hunts down 1st place
  • Specials: every racer has their own ability. The meter under your item fills over time (faster from ? boxes). When it glows, press E.
